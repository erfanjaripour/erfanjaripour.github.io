#### Situation
Working with an open dataset for the Stroop interference project revealed unexpected data quality and preprocessing issues.

#### Data
The dataset contained incorrectly included header rows and duplicate participant entries across multiple trials. Multiple restructuring steps were required before analysis could proceed.

#### Inferenc
Open datasets require explicit preprocessing validation before any exploratory or statistical analysis. Without this step, early-stage results may be systematically biased or misleading.

#### Action
Develop a standardized preprocessing checklist to validate dataset structure before initiating any analysis.
